Course description

Hands-on laboratory in engineering graphics: sketching, orthographic projection, sectional and auxiliary views, dimensioning and tolerancing, assembly drawings, and solid modelling with a parametric CAD package.

Learning outcomes

  • Produce freehand pictorial and orthographic sketches of machine parts.
  • Construct sectional and auxiliary views to standard conventions.
  • Dimension and tolerance a drawing so that it can be manufactured.
  • Build parametric solid models and generate drawings from them.
  • Assemble parts into a working model and produce a bill of materials.
