Course description

Properties of fluids; fluid statics and pressure measurement; the control-volume forms of mass, momentum and energy; the Bernoulli equation; dimensional analysis and similitude; viscous flow in pipes and head losses; external flow, drag and lift; and an introduction to turbomachinery.

Learning outcomes

  • Compute hydrostatic forces on submerged plane and curved surfaces.
  • Apply conservation of mass, momentum and energy to control volumes.
  • Use dimensional analysis and similitude to plan and interpret model tests.
  • Size pipes and pumps by evaluating major and minor losses in internal flow.
  • Estimate drag and lift on bodies in external flow.
